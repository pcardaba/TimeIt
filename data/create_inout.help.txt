create_inout  -name inout_name
              [-specify (internal)|external]

              # Input part (the port is driven from outside) :
              -in_launch_clock launch_clk
              -in_capture_clock capture_clk
              [-rclk_inputdly_max {indly_expr}]
              [-rclk_inputdly_min {indly_expr}]
              [-fclk_inputdly_max {indly_expr}]
              [-fclk_inputdly_min {indly_expr}]
              [-in_rclk_latency_max {lat_expr}]
              [-in_rclk_latency_min {lat_expr}]
              [-in_fclk_latency_max {lat_expr}]
              [-in_fclk_latency_min {lat_expr}]
              [-in_data_edges {list_edges}]
              [-in_hiz_edges {list_edges}]
              [-in_high_edges {list_edges}]
              [-in_low_edges {list_edges}]
              [-in_unknown_edges {list_edges}]

              # Output part (the port is driven by the device) :
              -out_launch_clock launch_clk
              -out_capture_clock capture_clk
              [-rclk_outputdly_max {outdly_expr}]
              [-rclk_outputdly_min {outdly_expr}]
              [-fclk_outputdly_max {outdly_expr}]
              [-fclk_outputdly_min {outdly_expr}]
              [-rclk_oedly_max {oedly_expr}]
              [-rclk_oedly_min {oedly_expr}]
              [-fclk_oedly_max {oedly_expr}]
              [-fclk_oedly_min {oedly_expr}]
              [-out_rclk_latency_max {lat_expr}]
              [-out_rclk_latency_min {lat_expr}]
              [-out_fclk_latency_max {lat_expr}]
              [-out_fclk_latency_min {lat_expr}]
              [-out_data_edges {list_edges}]
              [-out_hiz_edges {list_edges}]
              [-out_high_edges {list_edges}]
              [-out_low_edges {list_edges}]
              [-out_unknown_edges {list_edges}]

              [-pulled_up]
              [-hide_contention]
              [-color (black)|green|red|blue|orange|purple]
              [-amplitude amp_value]
              [-lwidth line_width]
              [-visible]
              [-help]


Creates a bidirectional (inout) signal: a device port that is driven from
outside at some times and by the device at others. It is "create_input" and
"create_output" in a single command: the signal has an INPUT PART and an
OUTPUT PART, each with its own launch/capture clocks, delays and launch edge
lists, drawn in the same waveform slot. The GUI calls it a "Bidir" signal.

The options of a part are the ones of create_input / create_output and mean
the same, see "create_input -help" and "create_output -help" for the details.
The delay options name their part by themselves (-rclk_inputdly_max,
-rclk_outputdly_max, -rclk_oedly_max). The options both commands share
(clocks, clock latencies, edge lists) take an "in_" or an "out_" prefix.

  inout_name     : Mandatory. The bidirectional signal (port) name.
  specify        : Optional. Choice: (default is "internal"). ONE choice for the whole port: both
                   parts are specified the same way.
                   internal   : Delays are those of your own flip-flops. Input part: setup/hold
                                of the capturing flip-flops, counted BACKWARDS from the CAPTURING
                                clock edge. Output part: clock to output of the launching
                                flip-flops, counted FORWARD from the LAUNCHING clock edge.
                   external   : Delays are those of the device at the other end, as the SDC
                                "set_input_delay" / "set_output_delay" constraints give them.
                                Input part: clock to output of the driving device, counted
                                FORWARD from the LAUNCHING clock edge. Output part: setup/hold
                                of the receiving device, counted BACKWARDS from the CAPTURING
                                clock edge.
  launch_clk     : Name of the clock that launches the data of the part. Mandatory for each part,
                   unless its capture clock is given. The edge lists of the part name edges of
                   this clock.
  capture_clk    : Name of the clock that captures the data of the part. Mandatory for each part,
                   unless its launch clock is given. Giving only one of both means that the
                   same clock launches and captures.
                   ALL the clocks of a bidirectional signal must be related: the launch and
                   capture clocks of the input part and the ones of the output part must share
                   the same source clock (they may be the same clock, a source clock and a clock
                   generated from it, or clocks generated from the same source clock).
  indly_expr     : Expression. Optional. Input delays of the input part, as in create_input.
                     -rclk_inputdly_max/min : max/min input delay at the RISING clock edge.
                     -fclk_inputdly_max/min : max/min input delay at the FALLING clock edge.
  outdly_expr    : Expression. Optional. Output delays of the output part, as in create_output.
                     -rclk_outputdly_max/min : max/min output delay at the RISING clock edge.
                     -fclk_outputdly_max/min : max/min output delay at the FALLING clock edge.
  oedly_expr     : Expression. Optional. Output enable delays of the output part, as in
                   create_output. Default is 0. Only relevant if "internal" -specify is used.
                   They time every change of the output part to or from Hi-Z, that is the
                   moment the device releases the port or takes it back.
  lat_expr       : Expression. Optional. Clock latency of the flip-flops of the part. Default is 0.
                   Only relevant if "internal" -specify is used.
                     -in_rclk_latency_max/min, -in_fclk_latency_max/min : capturing flip-flops
                                                                          of the input part.
                     -out_rclk_latency_max/min, -out_fclk_latency_max/min : launching flip-flops
                                                                          of the output part.
  list_edges     : List of launch clock edges of the part, as in create_input / create_output.
                   Simple number refers to any clock edge (rising or falling).
                   Compound NumberLetter refers to cycle and edge. "1P": Cycle 1 Posedge.
		               ___    ___
		   Example: __/   \__/   \___/
		           0  1   2  3   4   5
			   0  1P  1N 2P  2N  3P

                   -in_data_edges / -out_data_edges : edges where data (multi-bit) is launched.
                   -in_hiz_edges / -out_hiz_edges   : edges where the part stops driving the port
                                                      (Hi-Z).
                   -in_high_edges / -in_low_edges, -out_high_edges / -out_low_edges : edges where
                                                      a single-bit signal goes high/low.
                   -in_unknown_edges / -out_unknown_edges : edges where the value becomes unknown.

                   IMPORTANT: a part that is not driving the port must be Hi-Z. While the port
                   receives, the output part must be Hi-Z (-out_hiz_edges). While the device
                   drives, the input part must be Hi-Z (-in_hiz_edges). The Hi-Z line is only
                   drawn where NO part drives the port.
  pulled_up      : Optional. The port has a pull-up (open-drain line): once released it rises
                   to the high level instead of floating. Both parts pulling the line low at
                   the same time is then not a contention.
  hide_contention : Optional. Do not mark the contention zones on the waveform (the warning is
                   still given).
  color          : Optional. Trace color. (black) | green| red | blue | orange | purple.
  amp_value      : Optional. Trace amplitude (integer value) in pixels. Amplitude shall be > 2.
                   Default is 40.
  line_width     : Optional. Trace line width in pixel (integer value). Default is 2.
  visible        : Optional. Trace is visible is -visible is given, hidden otherwise.

Contention:

  Where both parts may drive the port at the same time there is a port contention. A part
  drives in every state but Hi-Z, transition windows included: a release that may end after
  the other side may already drive is a contention (this is what a bus turnaround time
  avoids). The zones are marked in red on the waveform and a warning lists them in the console.
  It is only a warning: the signal is created as given, since showing a contention may be the
  purpose of the diagram.

SDC:

  write_sdc constrains a bidirectional port as both an input and an output: SDC has no
  statement of its own for it. The input part gives a set_input_delay and the output part a
  set_output_delay, both on the port inout_name.

Examples:


1.
   create_inout -name "dq<7:0>" \
                -specify external \
                -in_launch_clock clk \
                -rclk_inputdly_max {$tCO_ext_max} \
                -rclk_inputdly_min {$tCO_ext_min} \
                -in_hiz_edges {0 6P} \
                -in_data_edges {4P 5P} \
                -out_launch_clock clk \
                -rclk_outputdly_max {$tSU_ext} \
                -rclk_outputdly_min {-$tH_ext} \
                -out_hiz_edges {0 3P} \
                -out_data_edges {1P 2P} \
                -visible

Creates a bidirectional bus launched and captured by "clk" in both directions. The device
drives two data words (launched at 1P and 2P) and releases the bus at 3P. The cycle starting
at 3P is the turnaround: nobody drives. The external device then drives two words (4P, 5P)
and releases the bus at 6P. Notice that each part is Hi-Z from edge 0 until it first drives,
and Hi-Z again once it is done.


2.
   create_inout -name sda \
                -specify external \
                -pulled_up \
                -in_launch_clock scl \
                -in_hiz_edges {0 10N} \
                -in_low_edges {9N} \
                -out_launch_clock scl \
                -out_hiz_edges {0 9N} \
                -out_low_edges {1N} \
                -visible

Creates an open-drain line with a pull-up. The device pulls it low at 1N and releases it at
9N, where the device at the other end pulls it low for one clock cycle (an acknowledge).
